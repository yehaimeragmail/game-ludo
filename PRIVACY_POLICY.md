# Privacy Policy — Ludo

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for Ludo. It is written in plain words on purpose, so anyone can understand it at a glance.

## About this game

Ludo is a classic board game for Apple devices (iOS). You play it on your own device, and it works offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the game.

## In short

- Ludo collects **no personal data at all**.
- There is **no account, no sign-in, and no login** of any kind.
- There is **no tracking**, **no third-party analytics**, and **no advertising** in the Apple version.
- Nothing you do in the game is sent to a server by this game.
- Your game progress, scores, and settings stay **on your device only**.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of Ludo:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
- Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

## What stays on your device only

The only things the game keeps are the things it needs to play on your own device, for example your game progress, your best scores, and your sound and display settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this game. If you delete the game from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

## Purchases

If Ludo offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This game does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks content inside the game itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

## Children

For younger players: this game collects nothing from children, just as it collects nothing from adults. There is no sign-in, no chat, and no place to share personal details.

## Changes to this policy

That covers everything. Should this policy ever change, the updated version will be posted at the same link first, and it will not reduce the protections described here without notice.
